Questions shop owners ask

Something else? Email us

Does Nuqta need internet?

No. Sales, stock and reports run on the PC. Internet is used only for updates and the daily copy to your cloud folder.

Which PC does Nuqta need?

A 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC. Any receipt printer, barcode scanner or cash drawer that works with Windows works with Nuqta.

Can two branches share one stock list?

Not yet. Each shop runs its own copy of Nuqta on its own PC.

What happens when the 14 days end?

Selling stops until you add a license key. Your products, customers, reports and backups stay and still open.

How much does a license cost?

Email us your kind of shop and how many PCs you sell from, and we reply with a price.
