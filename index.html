How it works

From the first scan to the month's profit

  1. 01

    Sell

    Scan, tap or search. Every line shows in dollars, the total in dollars and lira.

  2. 02

    Take payment

    Dollars, lira or both. Lira rounds to the nearest 5,000 and the change splits the way the customer asks.

  3. 03

    Keep the debt book

    Put a sale on a customer's account and send the reminder on WhatsApp in one click.

  4. 04

    Count stock as you sell

    Barcodes, scale labels and boxes take stock down at the till. Low items show before the shelf is empty.

  5. 05

    Close the shift

    The cashier counts the drawer in both currencies. The Z report shows any difference.

  6. 06

    See the profit

    Profit month by month after rent, salaries and the generator bill. Save the year to Excel.

Features

Everything the shop needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Each kind of shop turns on its own tools at setup. Here is what comes with Nuqta.

Delivery orders

Address, fee and driver on every order, and a slip printed for the bag.

Gift cards

Sell them in dollars, spend them over many visits, check the balance any time.

Barcodes and scales

Print shelf labels, read scale barcodes and sell by the box or the piece.

Staff with PINs

Each cashier signs in with a PIN. Discounts and refunds need a manager's approval.

Daily backup

A copy of the shop goes to OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox every day.

Updates in one click

New versions arrive inside the app. Your data stays where it is.

Arabic and English

Switch the language from the side menu, at any time, per cashier.

Works offline

Sales, stock and reports live on the PC. No internet, no problem.

Set the rate once each morning. Every price, total and receipt follows it, in dollars and in lira.
